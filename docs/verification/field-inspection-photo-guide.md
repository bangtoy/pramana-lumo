# Dump truck photo and operator-report guide

This guide uses the supplied `02-Dump-Truck-Tronton-U-Type` assets. The photos
were inspected and used unchanged in real-agent simulations. This is guidance
for documenting checks, not a certification of the vehicle pictured.

| Supplied Normal photo | What the photo documents | Useful improvement / direct account |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Cabin and safety | Seat belt, mirrors, mounted extinguisher and cabin context | The extinguisher label/gauge is small and partly obstructed. A close-up would improve readability. The inspector can report the gauge zone, expiry, seal, belt and horn checks directly. |
| 02 Brakes | Dashboard and gauges in a wide cabin view | Frame the gauge and warning indicators more closely when taking another photo. A still image cannot prove parking/service brake performance or absence of air leaks; report the actual stationary test results. |
| 03 Tyres and wheels | Visible sidewalls, tread and wheel nuts on the pictured wheels | Usable documentation. The inspector reports checking the remaining wheels and both sides; one representative photo does not show every tyre. |
| 04 Dump body | Body floor and general condition | Usable overview. Report the weld/mounting inspection; photograph a particular crack or suspect mounting if present. |
| 05 Hydraulic cylinder | Cylinder rod, gland and hose connections | Usable documentation. Report other hoses/seals checked from a safe position. Do not approach a leak or an unsupported raised body for a better angle. |
| 06 Body safety prop | Prop, mounting areas and locking hardware | The supplied normal image is useful. Report actual seating and locking checks from a safe position; visible damage or disengagement cannot be dismissed as “normal”. |
| 07 Tailgate | Latch and surrounding surfaces | Usable documentation. Directly report whether both latches close and lock; surface rust alone does not establish a failed latch. |
| 08 Lights and reflectors | Illuminated rear lamps and reflective markings in one state | Usable documentation. Confirm actual headlight, brake-light and indicator tests. A different still photo still cannot show every lighting function. |

The complete Normal and Issue simulations each submitted all eight items using
the eight original photos, with no replacement-photo request after the direct
inspection observations were supplied. Recreating the whole Normal photo set
was therefore unnecessary for this dataset. Better extinguisher/gauge close-ups
would improve documentation, while functional checks still need an operator
account.

Short example inputs used successfully:

- Cabin: “Kabin normal. Sabuk mengunci, spion utuh, klakson bunyi; APAR segel
  utuh, gauge hijau dan belum kedaluwarsa.”
- Brakes: “Rem dites diam sesuai SOP: tekanan operasi normal, low air padam,
  tidak bocor, rem parkir dan servis normal.”
- Lights: “Lampu depan, rem kiri-kanan dan semua sein sudah dites normal;
  reflektor utuh.”

These are examples of reports from checks actually completed by the inspector.
When a check has not been performed safely, choose “I could not check safely”.
The simulation supplied scripted operator observations; it did not perform
physical tests on a real truck.

Small text and incorrect visual descriptions are documented vision limitations
in [OpenAI's image-input guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/images-vision).
