One workflow from preparation to follow-up

What you can do

Build inspection plans

Upload a specification sheet and refine the checks with Lumo. Locate inspection points on an interactive isometric model.

Inspect on a phone

Follow the inspection conversation, take photos or attach gallery images, and submit findings with supporting evidence.

Review results

See inspection status, urgent findings, trends, and recurring issues. Search reports, review evidence, and export PDFs.

Manage follow-up

Create Work Orders from findings, assign responsibility, set deadlines, and record completion while preserving the inspection verdict.

Ask Lumo

Ask questions across reports and Work Orders, open cited sources, and review recommendations. The read-only analyst provides a labelled data-based fallback.
