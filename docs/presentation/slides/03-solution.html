The solution

Planning through inspection

Lumo turns requirements into inspection checks, guides the conversation, and requests the evidence needed to complete them.

Findings connected to their evidence

Interactive models locate the parts being inspected. Findings and photos stay attached to their reports.

Review and follow-up

Work Orders give follow-up an owner and deadline. Supervisors ask Lumo questions and open cited sources.
