Scattered context and incomplete evidence

The problem

Collecting and assembling the report

Inspection and survey work spans checklists, photos, chat messages, and spreadsheets.

People record observations in the field, explain them elsewhere, and manually assemble a report afterward. Scattered context makes completeness and supporting evidence hard to assess.

Reviewing and acting on findings

Reviewers need to spot urgent issues, compare past results, and decide what needs attention.

Missing photos create extra trips. Recurring problems are easy to overlook. Findings can sit in a report without a clear owner or next step.
