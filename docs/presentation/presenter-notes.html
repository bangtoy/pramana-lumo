02 · 45 seconds

Incomplete reports create more work for supervisors

00:15–01:00. “Sometimes an operator sends a message like ‘Truck checked, all good.’ But which components were checked? Where are the photos? If something is wrong, what needs attention? Reports can arrive in different formats, with missing details or evidence. The supervisor then spends time asking follow-up questions and reconstructing the inspection. Our problem is the work needed to turn that incomplete field input into a report that can actually be reviewed.” The message is an illustrative example, not a customer quotation. Do not attach invented frequency or time-saving figures.

03 · 30 seconds

Lumo turns field input into reports and Work Orders

01:00–01:30. “The field operator reports through Lumo. Instead of passing missing information to the supervisor, Lumo asks for the required details and evidence, then organizes the result into a structured report. When a finding requires action, it also generates a Work Order. For example, a flat tyre can produce a task to purchase a replacement. The supervisor can monitor both the report and the Work Order.” Work Order generation is the user-confirmed product workflow. Do not describe generation of a purchasing task as an executed purchase.

05 · 40 seconds

Inspection context connects evidence to action

03:00–03:40. “The difference is the context carried through the workflow. The vehicle and component define the questions. The quality of the evidence determines the follow-up. The finding then supplies context for a generated Work Order, such as purchasing a replacement tyre. Code enforces required results and evidence, while the supervisor monitors reports and tasks. This is why we fit Deep Domain AI and AI-Native Products & Operations.” Keep the technical explanation brief. A complete report is not an automatic assurance that the vehicle is safe.

06 · 40 seconds

Less chasing, more time for review and follow-up

03:40–04:20. “For the operator, the benefit is knowing what information to provide. For the supervisor, it is a report ready for review and visibility into the Work Orders that follow. The intended time saving comes from reducing repeated questions and manual report cleanup. We will test that in a pilot by measuring completeness at submission, supervisor review time, clarification requests, and the time from a finding to a Work Order. These are proposed measures, not results we have already achieved.”

This gives us a repeatable way to build around the inspection workflow.” Reference the available tickets and artifacts.
