P2H backed by evidence

02 · 25 seconds

A checkmark does not show what was inspected

00:20–00:45. “A completed form may not provide enough information for a decision. Each vehicle has specific components. Photos can be too distant. And each finding needs someone responsible for follow-up. These are the three gaps we focus on.” The problem comes from the PRD. Do not cite error rates, accident statistics, or interview findings that are not available.

03 · 25 seconds

One workflow from specifications to action

00:45–01:10. “The supervisor prepares a plan from the specifications and P2H Standard. The inspector receives guidance on collecting evidence. The results become a report and a Work Order. Lumo helps assess input, while code enforces completeness. The supervisor remains responsible for actions.” Guide the audience from left to right without reading every box.

04 · 35 seconds

The plan follows the vehicle being inspected

01:10–01:45. “Here, the supervisor prepares a dump truck inspection plan. Selecting the tire item can highlight the component on the Vehicle Model. Each item explains what to check, which photos to collect, and when to STOP. The supervisor can complete and edit the plan.” For a live demo, use an existing seeded plan. Latency for generating a new plan has not been measured. Keep tab switching brief to stay within 35 seconds.

05 · 50 seconds

Clearer evidence while the inspector is still on site

01:45–02:35. Click 1, then 2, then 3. “The fire extinguisher is visible, but its label is unreadable. The conversation asks for a closer photo. The label photo improves the evidence, but does not establish that the extinguisher is safe. The tire bulge matches a STOP rule. The finding needs evidence and escalation to the supervisor.” State that this is a scripted workflow simulation with sample photos. Allow about 12 seconds per stage and 14 seconds to explain the code safeguards.

06 · 40 seconds

One finding through to follow-up

02:35–03:15. Show the report, click Work Order, then the Ask Lumo reference if time permits. “The supervisor sees the component, photo, and reason for STOP. A Work Order assigns the finding to a mechanic with a deadline. Handling status stays synchronized with the report. In the P3 design, Ask Lumo includes sources.” Do not create or close WOs on the shared deployment just for the presentation. Closing a WO does not automatically establish roadworthiness. The live version may use the name Pak Pramana. Lumo branding and some dashboard features come from the supplied design references.

07 · 30 seconds

Agents assess input, code enforces rules

03:15–03:45. “Reliability extends beyond the prompt. Code rejects results without required photos, tracks progress, and blocks incomplete submissions. If the Analyst fails, fallback answers still refer to the data. The supervisor controls actions.” Source evidence comes from the lumo-design branch. Do not claim that all these scenarios have just passed end-to-end testing on the live server.

08 · 25 seconds

Evidence, components, and actions share context

03:45–04:10. “Our insight is that useful evidence depends on the component under inspection. The plan, follow-up questions, and corrective actions therefore share the same vehicle context. We build report quality during the inspection, while the inspector is still on site.” Do not claim that no other product does this.

09 · 25 seconds

Benefits that can be assessed and measured

04:10–04:35. “The benefits differ by role: guidance for inspectors, evidence ready for supervisor review, and clear repair tasks for mechanics. The PRD targets plan creation in under 30 seconds and eight-item P2H in under four minutes. Both remain targets, with operational impact still to be measured.”

Deep Domain AI for dump truck P2H

04:35–04:55. “Our strongest fit is Deep Domain AI because we model the actual P2H workflow. The AI-native approach appears in conversations that guide evidence collection. We deliberately limit autonomy: the supervisor still reviews plans and decides on actions.”

Appendix: agent and code boundaries

Appendix for architecture questions. Name one agent per responsibility and explain the separation between dialogue and deterministic rules. Do not present configured model versions as public release facts: repo model names may be local endpoint aliases. Generating a Vehicle Model requires a Linux runtime according to the ADR. Ready-made models support the demo.

Appendix · outside the 6 minutes

Appendix: what the evidence establishes

Describe the status precisely: read-only captures establish page accessibility and visible content. They do not establish that the entire agent workflow works. Design references, branch source, and deployment captures are different types of evidence. Closing a Work Order records handling, without automatically authorizing vehicle operation.
