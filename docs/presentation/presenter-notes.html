01 · 40 seconds

What if inspections, surveys, and reporting were supported by a proactive AI agent?

00:00–00:40. “What if inspections, surveys, and reporting were supported by a proactive AI agent? An agent that helps prepare the right checks, asks for missing evidence, and brings important findings to your attention as you work. One that keeps observations, photos, reports, and follow-up connected from start to finish. That is the idea behind Pramana and Lumo.” Use the inspection workspace image to introduce the concrete product.

02 · 55 seconds

The problem

00:40–01:35. “Inspection and survey work often spans a checklist, a photo gallery, chat messages, and a spreadsheet. People collect information in the field, explain it elsewhere, and then assemble the report. Reviewers have to work out whether a check is complete and whether its evidence supports the finding. The work continues after submission. Missing photos can mean another trip. Recurring problems can be missed. A finding can remain in a report without a clear owner or next step.” Explain the operator and supervisor effort using the README’s problem framing.

03 · 55 seconds

The solution

01:35–02:30. “Pramana brings this idea to an evidence-based inspection workflow. Lumo helps turn requirements into checks, guides the inspection in conversation, and asks for the evidence needed to complete the checks. Interactive visual models connect each check to the relevant part. Findings and photos stay attached to reports. Work Orders give the follow-up an owner and deadline. Supervisors can ask Lumo about the evidence, follow its source links, and review recommendations grounded in the recorded data. People remain responsible for review and action.”

04 · 55 seconds

What you can do

02:30–03:25. “There are five things you can do. Build a plan from a specification sheet and locate the checks on the model. Inspect on a phone and submit photo evidence. Review results, urgent findings, and trends, with searchable reports and PDF export. Manage follow-up through Work Orders with assigned responsibility and deadlines. And ask Lumo questions across reports and Work Orders with source links and recommendations. The analyst is read-only and can use a clearly labelled data-based fallback.” Transition directly to the application demonstration.

06 · 35 seconds

Technology

04:55–05:30. “Pramana uses Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and TanStack Query. The agents run through the OpenAI Agents SDK for JavaScript and TypeScript, with Zod validation and server-enforced inspection rules. SQLite stores application data, alongside uploads, generated models, and local agent traces. The Anatomy skill supplies the geometry workflow for interactive SVG models. The interface and assistant replies use English, and operational dates use Jakarta time.” Reserve the remaining 30 seconds for app transitions or response time.
