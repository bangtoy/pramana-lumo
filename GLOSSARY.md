# Pramana

Pramana supports field operators and supervisors in collecting and reviewing vehicle inspection reports. These are the canonical English terms for the product pitch.

## Language

**Pre-operational inspection**:
An inspection of a vehicle before it enters operation, carried out by a field operator.
_Avoid_: P2H, daily check, pre-start check

**Field operator**:
The person who inspects the physical vehicle and reports observations with supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: inspector, user, field staff

**Supervisor**:
The person who prepares inspection requirements, reviews inspection reports, and decides on follow-up actions.
_Avoid_: admin, manager

**Inspection report**:
A record of one field operator's inspection of one vehicle, containing observations and supporting evidence for the inspected components.
_Avoid_: form, checklist, chat history
